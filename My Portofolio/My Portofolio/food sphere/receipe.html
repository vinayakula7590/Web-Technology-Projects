👨‍🍳 How to Make Samosa

Learn how to make delicious samosa at home.

🏠 Home Style Samosa

Difficulty: Easy

Preparation: 30 minutes

Cooking: 20 minutes

🧂 Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 3 potatoes
  • 1/2 cup peas
  • Green chilli
  • Ginger
  • Cumin
  • Garam masala
  • Salt
  • Oil

👨‍🍳 Steps

  1. Prepare the dough.
  2. Boil and mash the potatoes.
  3. Prepare the spiced filling.
  4. Roll the dough into small circles.
  5. Fold the dough into a cone.
  6. Fill the cone with the potato mixture.
  7. Seal the samosa.
  8. Fry until golden and crispy.
  9. Serve hot with chutney.

🍳 Restaurant Style

For a restaurant-style result, focus on a crisp, well-sealed pastry and balanced seasoning.

Chef Tips

  • Do not make the dough too soft.
  • Keep the filling relatively dry.
  • Seal the edges properly.
  • Fry at an appropriate temperature.

👨‍🍳 5-Star Chef Style

Create a refined presentation with carefully seasoned filling, crisp pastry and fresh chutneys.

Presentation

  • Serve on a clean plate.
  • Add mint chutney.
  • Add tamarind chutney.
  • Garnish with fresh coriander.
